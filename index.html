00Manifesto

Kujira, japanese for whale, is what happens when one person keeps building tools for their own life and refuses to stop shipping them.

No investors, no roadmap meetings, no team standup. Just a founder who wanted better software for his own collection, his own money, his own trading habit and his own notes, and decided the fastest way to get it was to build it himself.

Every Kujira app is a single-page site. No framework, no bundler, no build step. Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, shipped straight to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. That is not a limitation Julian works around, it is the method. A framework buys velocity on a team. Solo, against four separate apps, the tax of learning and maintaining someone else's abstraction costs more than it saves.

The constraint forces a kind of honesty. If a feature is slow, there is no library to blame. If a screen is confusing, there is no design system hiding the decision. Every line is a decision Julian made and can explain. That is the vanilla thesis, and it is the reason four very different apps, tracking cards, net worth, a single stock, and a private journal, can all trace back to the same hand.

Abyss is the map of that work. Four ventures, four depths, one descent. Scroll down.

01Sunlight zone

Collectibles

Epipelagic
0m → 200m

A Pokemon card collectibles tracker, built as a single-page PWA. This is where the whole brand started, close enough to the surface to still see daylight through the water.

Collectibles tracks singles, graded slabs and sealed product side by side, feeds an eBay listing pipeline, keeps a record of every sale, and pulls live market pricing from TCGdex and PricePerToken so a valuation is never more than a page refresh stale. It runs on vanilla JS with a Supabase backend, fronted by a Cloudflare Worker proxy for the pricing calls.

  • Singles, graded slabs and sealed product tracked in one view
  • eBay listing pipeline and sales record built in
  • Live pricing from TCGdex and PricePerToken
  • Currently v3.26

02Twilight zone

Portfolio

Mesopelagic
200m → 1,000m

A personal net worth tracker. Past the sunlight zone, the light thins and the stakes go up, this is where the money question gets asked properly.

Portfolio pulls together SGX and US stocks, crypto, real estate, cash and CPF, Singapore's state retirement fund, and reports all of it in one currency, SGD. It carries salary tracking, profit and loss, and a FIRE projection, and insurance policies live inside it as a tab rather than a separate app. Backend is Google Sheets via Apps Script, because a spreadsheet is a perfectly good database when you are the only user.

  • SGX, US, crypto, real estate, cash and CPF in one SGD figure
  • Salary tracking, profit and loss, FIRE projection
  • Insurance policies as a tab, not a separate app
  • Currently v2.43

03Midnight zone

Trading

Bathypelagic
1,000m → 4,000m

A single-file day trading dashboard, no daylight left, everything moves fast and the read has to be instant.

Trading is focused on one stock, Micron Technology under ticker MU. It draws a live price feed and a candlestick chart, then layers VWAP, EMA, RSI and MACD on top. The data is read-only, there is no order execution here, it is a lens on the market, not a way to touch it.

  • Single-file dashboard, focused on MU only
  • Candlestick chart with VWAP, EMA, RSI and MACD
  • Read-only market data, no order execution

04The abyss

Journal

Abyssopelagic
4,000m → 6,000m

A personal documents app. This is the deepest layer, the one nobody else reads, which is exactly why it is the one built with the most care.

Journal is single user by design, cloud-primary on Supabase rather than local-first, with a block editor and realtime sync so a thought written on one device is already there on the next. It is live at kujira-journal.pages.dev. Of the four ventures it asks the least of the visitor and the most of the founder, there is nowhere to hide a bad sentence when you are the only reader.

  • Single user, cloud-primary on Supabase
  • Block editor with realtime sync
  • Live at kujira-journal.pages.dev

05Depth log

The build history

A logbook, not a highlight reel. Entries are ordered by how deep they sit in the descent, not by date, each one is a real milestone in how Kujira got built.

Entry 01
The vanilla thesis

No framework, on purpose

Every Kujira app is hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No React, no build step, no node_modules. The rule was set before Collectibles existed and has held for all four ventures since.

Entry 02
Collectibles v1.0 → v3.26

Where the brand started

Began as a plain list of singles. Grew a grading module, condition tracking, an eBay listing pipeline and a sales record, then live pricing from TCGdex and PricePerToken through a Cloudflare Worker proxy. Now a full PWA on Supabase.

Entry 03
Portfolio v1.0 → v2.43

The money question, answered once

Started as a spreadsheet Julian was already keeping. Rebuilt as an app on top of the same Google Sheet via Apps Script, so SGX, US stocks, crypto, real estate, cash and CPF finally sit in one SGD figure with salary, P&L and a FIRE projection attached.

Entry 04
Trading, single file

One stock, no build step

A day trading dashboard for MU, shipped as one file. Candlestick chart, VWAP, EMA, RSI and MACD, entirely read-only. No order execution was ever in scope.

Entry 05
Journal, live

Cloud-primary from day one

The newest venture and the only one built cloud-first from the start rather than migrated to it. Single user, a block editor, realtime sync on Supabase, live at kujira-journal.pages.dev.

Entry 06
Abyss, this site

The brand gets a home

Kujira never had a site, a mark or a stated identity before this one. Abyss is that identity, built the same way as everything else it describes, one page, vanilla, self-contained.

06Hadal zone

Below the floor

The hadal zone, the trenches, is the deepest water there is. Kujira has four ventures mapped so far. Whatever comes next files here, when it exists and not before.
